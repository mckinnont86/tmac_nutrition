When Things Get Hard
Protocols & Rules
Plateau protocol: If weight stalls for 2+ weeks, drop carbs by 20g first — reduce the rice or potato portion. Never cut protein. Try this for 2 weeks before any other adjustment.
Performance dips: If strength or run pace drops significantly for 2+ consecutive weeks, bump to 1,800 cal temporarily. Don't grind through impaired form — that's how injuries happen.
Scale expectations: Week 1–2: 3–5 lbs (mostly water + glycogen — don't get overconfident). Weeks 3+: 1.5–2.5 lbs/week average. Some flat weeks are completely normal.
Desk discipline: Eat at a table, not at the screen. Drink 16 oz water before reaching for food between meals — most mid-desk hunger is thirst or boredom, not real hunger.
Batch cook Sunday: Brown a pound of ground beef, cook a batch of rice, roast potato wedges. Lunch is solved for 3 days. Remove the friction.
Ice cream rule: Up to 3× per week in the evening slot. ½ cup ice cream or 2 cookies both land ~150 cal. More = trades against dinner's rice or potato, not in addition to it.

Hydration
Baseline: half your bodyweight in oz. At 240 lbs that's 120 oz minimum on a desk-only day. Every training session adds to that — 16–24 oz per hour of running, 8–16 oz for a lift session.
Creatine demands more water. 10g creatine daily pulls water into muscle cells. Skimping on water while on creatine causes headaches, cramping, and blunts the benefits. This is the most common creatine mistake.
Travel days: drink significantly more. Jerky, cheese packs, pretzels, and Chex mix are all high sodium. The scale will be up the next morning — that's water retention, not fat. Offset it with 150+ oz on travel days.
Real-time check: urine color. Pale yellow = good. Dark yellow = drink more. Clear = slightly overdone but fine. Use a 40 oz Stanley or Hydro Flask — 3 fills on desk days, 4 on training days.
Monster Energy: Sugar-free only on this plan — same 160mg caffeine, ~10 cal vs. 190 cal and 54g sugar in a regular can. Keep it before noon (caffeine half-life is 5–6 hrs and will affect sleep). Offset each can with an extra 16 oz of water. Three sugar-free Monsters a week is a non-issue for your goals.
Sleep
7.5 hrs is your floor — not your target. Sleep cycles run ~90 minutes. 7.5 hrs (5 cycles) and 9 hrs (6 cycles) feel better than 8 hrs which cuts mid-cycle and leaves you groggy.
Poor sleep works directly against the cut. Sleep deprivation raises cortisol (increases fat storage, breaks down muscle), spikes ghrelin (hunger hormone up ~24%), and drops willpower. You will be hungrier and make worse food choices on bad sleep nights — especially hard at 1,750 cal.
Growth hormone releases primarily in deep sleep. That's when your body actually repairs the muscle from lifting and the connective tissue stress from running. The work you put in is only as good as the sleep you get after it. Creatine and collagen both do more work during sleep — chronically short nights waste them.
Protect bedtime on early run days. If you're out the door at 6am, that means lights out by 10–10:30pm. Don't let late nights stack on top of early mornings — that's the fastest way to stall progress and increase injury risk on the half marathon plan.
Heavy training days (long run or run + lift): Prioritize 9 hrs. Your body needs the extra cycle. Non-negotiable during peak half marathon mileage weeks.

On The Go
Snacks & Travel Foods
Snacks are not a guaranteed slot. They replace the mid-morning optional or evening optional when you need something and a real meal isn't available. They don't add on top of the daily structure.
Traveling: Beef jerky is your best travel protein. Keep it in the bag. Pair with a small carb if you need a proper mini-meal, otherwise it's a standalone snack.
